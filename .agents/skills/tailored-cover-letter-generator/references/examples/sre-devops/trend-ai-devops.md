Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Applied AI DevOps Engineer role at Trend Micro. The role's emphasis on cloud delivery automation, observability, and repeatable AI-native development workflows aligns with my AWS platform practice and hands-on agent-tooling work. I am particularly interested in reducing engineering toil while keeping generated changes reviewable and human-owned.

Together, these experiences support your need for reusable automation and disciplined AI-assisted engineering practices.

I would bring cloud-native delivery foundations, deliberate agent-workflow design, and a strong review discipline to Trend Micro. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I could contribute to the team's AI-enabled DevOps workflows and service engineering.

Sincerely,
Jia-Long Lin
