## English

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the DevOps Engineer role at OpenNet. The role's emphasis on AWS and Kubernetes infrastructure, CI/CD improvement, environment provisioning, and operational visibility aligns with my cloud-native platform work and automation discipline. I am particularly interested in making infrastructure changes reproducible, reviewable, and easier for engineering teams to operate.

In Hiraya E-commerce, I designed a Terraform-managed AWS EKS platform with GitOps workflows and automated full deployment and destruction through GitHub Actions, ECR, Argo CD, and Kustomize. In pi-noid, I turned recurring AI-agent development tasks into reusable TypeScript tooling with explicit context management, CLI/MCP tool routing, and human review gates. Together, these experiences support your need for structured automation and maintainable workflows across platform delivery.

I would bring AWS EKS practice, infrastructure-as-code and GitOps foundations, and a deliberate approach to reusable automation to OpenNet. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I could contribute to the team's infrastructure and deployment platform.

Sincerely,
Jia-Long Lin
