## English

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the IT Site Reliability Engineer role at TSMC. The role's emphasis on operational automation, sustainable applications, Kubernetes, and observable systems aligns with my hands-on platform work and repeatable engineering workflow design. I am particularly interested in turning recurring operational work into reviewable tools and clearer diagnostic foundations.

In Hiraya E-commerce, I automated AWS EKS platform deployment and destruction through GitHub Actions, Terraform, ECR, Argo CD, and Kustomize, while establishing Prometheus and Grafana observability foundations for diagnosis and decision-making. In pi-noid, I built reusable TypeScript tooling for loop workflows, orchestration, and skill management, applying explicit context management, CLI/MCP tool routing, and human review gates across development tasks. Together, these experiences support your need for disciplined automation and software-engineering practices around operational tooling.

I would bring Kubernetes platform practice, observability foundations, and a systematic approach to automation to TSMC. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I could contribute to the team's reliability and operational engineering work.

Sincerely,
Jia-Long Lin
