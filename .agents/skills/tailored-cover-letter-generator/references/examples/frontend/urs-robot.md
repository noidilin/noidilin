## English

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Web Frontend Engineer role at URS Robot. The role's emphasis on turning robot monitoring, mapping, and dispatch data into responsive operational interfaces aligns with my work as a frontend engineer building high-density dashboards and predictable user workflows. I am particularly interested in helping engineers and customers act on complex device state through clear web experiences.

In the Misegoto project, I rebuilt a Japanese B2B admin dashboard with Next.js 16 and React 19, implementing filtering, export, batch actions, permission-aware interactions, responsive layouts, and typed API flows. In Hiraya E-commerce, I designed a Terraform-managed AWS EKS platform with GitOps workflows and established Prometheus and Grafana observability foundations for diagnosis and decision-making. Together, these experiences support your need for maintainable monitoring interfaces complemented by practical awareness of the systems and operational signals behind them.

I would bring React and TypeScript product delivery, operational dashboard experience, and complementary platform awareness to URS Robot. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I could contribute to the robot management platform.

Sincerely,
Jia-Long Lin
